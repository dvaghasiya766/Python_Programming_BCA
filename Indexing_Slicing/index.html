✂️ Step 4 — Explore String Slicing

🧠 Test Your Understanding

📚 Remember

Indexing

Accesses a single character.

text[2]
Slicing

Extracts a sequence of characters.

text[1:4]
Start

Included in the slice.

Stop

Excluded from the slice.

Step

Controls the jump between indexes.

Negative Index

Starts counting from the end.
